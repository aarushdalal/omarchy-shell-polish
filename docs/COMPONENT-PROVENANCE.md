# Component provenance

Omarchy wrappers and community plugins adapt the installed Omarchy UI, under
the upstream MIT terms included in OMARCHY-LICENSE. Modified 2026-10-11 for
calibrated motion, lifecycle and confirmation surfaces. Native focus and IPC
aliases are preserved. See https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy.

Caelestia overlays derive from https://github.com/caelestia-dots/shell and are
GPL-3.0-only, with corresponding QML source and terms in caelestia/LICENSE.
The independent motion provider derives from the MIT community motion engine.
The repository-level MIT license covers independent original snippets; it does
not replace the GPL terms on adapted Caelestia components.

Canonical Omarchy wrappers are copied into Focus, Cloud and System Pulse for
independent installation. Keep those copies byte-identical.

The transparent tech GIF is CC BY-SA 4.0 by Gustavo Girardelli, derived from Geek3. It remains under those terms; see caelestia/assets/CREDITS.md. The shared SessionActionState is independent MIT code.
